Stone of Alliance (SOA)

The SOA buff can be bought for 7,500 Guild Coins from the Guild NPC inside the guild base.

Noblesse Buff

Noblesse Buff is given to the biggest spender of each month (VVIP3). The NB holder decides the time and location of buffing once a day; when the buff is used, all players in the same town will receive it. Usually the holder will announce every day the time and location of the buff — you may ask others in chat or on Discord servers for NB info if you missed the announcement.

It is ideal to run dungeons, nests, GGC and Sunset Coliseum during this time to receive additional loot. (Common time and town for the NB buff is during night time GMT+8, 9PM–11PM at Saint Haven.)
