Leveling Up

Leveling with main quest

  • 1. Leveling become super easy because the EXP is consistent from lvl 1 to 95 (10.000 EXP for level up), it takes around 3-5 hours total.

  • 2. Only main quest can give you EXP (sometimes you don't have to clear the dungeon for the quest), clearng the quest gives you 600 ftg.

  • 3. Side quest does not give you EXP (but they give you jelly, useful for 95 later)

  • 4. It doesn't matter what difficulty you choose, the exp given from dungeon is only 1% (more on higher difficulty)

  • 5. Right at the start use the laugh gesture then equip the ring to get the newbie growth costume.

  • 6. There is possibility you can reach 95 but the main quest is still going (my cases is i reach 95 when my main quest is still lvl 70)

Leveling with Sparta Goblin

  • If you have BST points to spare, you could opt for leveling using the Sparta Goblins.

  • Ticket for the Sparta Goblin can be purchased from the BST store.

  • The Sparta Goblin portal is inside the Wonderful Themepark portal. The exp given by the Sparta Goblin depends on the difficulty level, but make sure to pick a difficulty that is approriate for your gear (usually LB 1/2).
