Leveling Up

Leveling with main quest

  1. Leveling becomes super easy because the EXP is consistent from level 1 to 95 (10,000 EXP for each level up). It takes around 3–5 hours total.
  2. Only main quests give you EXP (sometimes you don't have to clear the dungeon for the quest). Clearing the quest gives you 600 FTG.
  3. Side quests do not give EXP (but they give jelly, useful for level 95 later).
  4. It doesn't matter what difficulty you choose — the EXP given from a dungeon is only 1% (more on higher difficulty).
  5. Right at the start, use the laugh gesture then equip the ring to get the newbie growth costume.
  6. It is possible to reach 95 while the main quest is still going (in my case I reached 95 when my main quest was still level 70).

Leveling with Sparta Goblin

If you have BST points to spare, you could opt for leveling using the Sparta Goblins.

Tickets for the Sparta Goblin can be purchased from the BST store.

The Sparta Goblin portal is inside the Wonderful Themepark portal. The EXP given by the Sparta Goblin depends on the difficulty level, but make sure to pick a difficulty that is appropriate for your gear (usually LB 1/2).
