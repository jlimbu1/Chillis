Conquest

  • Conquest

    Conquest conquest, everyone's talking about conquest, but what is it? Let's find out.

    Conquest is a Guild Nest that is ran together by other fellow guild members to deal as much damage as possible in 3 minutes to 1 of the many bosses. The highest damage out of all your runs will then be calculated to the Conquest leaderboard in the Guilds tab, and the total amount added up.

  • Conquest Requirements

    1) All of the members in the party has to be the same guild as you!

    2) Conquest Ticket which can be obtained from Palace Attendant Jeokwolyang (Can only buy once a day)

  • Conquest Rewards

    A higher total damage will equals more rewards! (Rewards are delivered after the end of each season)

    ***In-order to recieve conquest rewards:

    • 1) MUST donate atleast 500 guild coins

    • 2) MUST run conquest atleast once

  • Conquest Talismans

    The most notable reward you can get from Conquest are Conquest Talismans and High Grade Unidentified Goddess Heraldry Boxes.

    But what are Conquest Talismans? Conquest Talismans are powerful talismans obtained from Conquest Rewards at the end of the season or through Conquest Missions that will increase certain stats HOWEVER will minus one specific stat. Like, literally.

  • Why run more than once?

    This is because the boss that spawns during the Conquest run has a random chance for its HP to range between 20% , 40% , 80% , 100%. This means you might deal less or more damage depending on whether you use Heavenly or Goddess Helmet!

    MOREOVER!! 3 random buffs of random levels will be given in a certain time interval one by one, and you may have a different party setup each time you run Conquest. Remember, every DPS counts!
