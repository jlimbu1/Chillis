Gold Farming

Sunset Coliseum

Using the Sunset Coliseum Map, you can enter the Sunset Coliseum located in Red Lotus Palace Main Street. There are 3 ways to get gold in the Sunset Coliseum: selling the sealed skill jades on the Trading House, selling the Goddess equipment to the NPC, and finally getting Gold Goblins.

The Goddess equipment sells for ~152 gold each and the Gold Goblins drop ~2000 gold.

Mission Boards

Doing special missions (Plunder, Coop, Alliance, Target, Counterattack, Treasure, Mining, Front Breakthrough) grants you 305g for completing them. It is recommended to run on multiple sub accounts to maximize the daily 3600 FTG given to every character. Doing this also allows you to sell those special missions to other players such as Coop, Alliance, Plunder, and Guild missions for additional gold.

Another way of farming gold through the mission board is by Tomb Hunting. To get the tomb invader, it is recommended that you have a general mission for that specific dungeon to increase the chance of its appearance. The tomb invader drops a random skill jade, and the fragments dropped can be used to craft sealed skill jades to sell.

Gold Goblin Coins

The GGC drop rate is currently 0.3% but will be increased to 1.8% in a future patch. In a normal run it should grant you 6000+ gold.
