Guild Missions

  • So you want to know more about how Guilds work? Then let's start with Guild Missions!

  • Guild Missions

    Guild Missions are cooperative missions that are done together with your fellow guildmates, which exchanges materials dropped in Dungeons, Nests and Special Instances in return for Guild Coins.

  • What are Guild Coins and Guild Shop?

    Guild Coins are currencies that are obtained through delivering materials to NPCs in Guild Missions. These coins can be used at the Guild Shop by talking to the NPC Guild Shopkeeper Jinin Saint Haven or the Guild Base (which will be discussed later) in Red Lotus Palace Main Street, for rewards such as Seal Stamps, Lagendia Torches, Food and more.

    Additional Guild Coins can be earnt by doing Guild Nests such as Push Crystal Statue (PCS) and Conquest with Mission.

  • Guild Contribution

    Furthermore, Guild Coins can also be donated to the Guild to help contribute towards Guild Reputation and accumulate Guild Points. Each coin donated is equal to 10 Guild Contribution & Guild Point, which are used to help purchase and maintain Guild Buffs, as well as level up the guild.

    In short, Guild Contribution is the total amount of Guild Coins you have donated multiplied by 10!

  • Guild Reputation & Conquest Season

    Every 2 weeks (17 days to be accurate), there will be a new Guild Season whereas all the existing Guilds in the server compete against their members' total Guild Contribution to become the BEST guild. A higher Guild Contribution than other guilds will mean a higher Guild Reputation, in which case would earn the Guild more rewards the higher the guild ranking.

    The TOP 16 Guilds that have secured their spot after the season has ended however, will be given a Guild Base which provides access to a special location at Red Lotus Palace Main Street, with facilities such as a 2nd Guild Shop and other necessities
