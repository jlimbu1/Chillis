Mission Board

The Mission Board is where you can find your missions, and it can be found in every town. There are different types of missions which each give different rewards, and they can be completed by finishing the designated Nests or Dungeons. In some cases, missions may be designated to a special instance such as Guild Nests.

There can only be a maximum of 6 missions at one time, and deleting a mission will put it into cooldown for 20 minutes (which can be instantly refreshed for Crystal Points). Upon completion, however, the mission will refresh without needing a time interval.

What are Hero's Blood Sweat Tears? [BST]

Blood Sweat Tears (commonly known as BST) are points that you obtain by completing missions that appear on your Mission Board. These points can be used to buy useful items at the Blood Sweat Tears Store, which is usually next to Mission Boards. Examples are at Saint Haven and Red Lotus Palace Main Street.

Hero's Blood Sweat Tears Store

The Blood Sweat Tear Store (BST Store) is where you can exchange the BST points you obtained from missions into several useful items, which are not limited to Potential Talisman Slots (used to expand your Talisman Slots), Horn of Life (extends your minion/pet duration) and many more.
