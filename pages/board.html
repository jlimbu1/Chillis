Mission Board

  • Mission Board

    The Mission Board is where you can find your missions and can be found at every town. There are different types of Missions which each give different rewards, and they can be completed by finishing the designated Nests or Dungeons; however in some cases, the missions may be designated to a special instance such as Guild Nests. There can only be a maximum of 6 missions at one time, and deleting a mission will make it go into cooldown for 20 minutes [Which can be instantly refreshed for Crystal Points.] However upon completion, the mission will refresh without needing a time interval.

  • What are Hero's Blood Sweat Tears? [BST]

    Blood Sweat Tears [commonly known as BST] are points that you obtain by completing missions that appear on your Mission Board. These points can be used to buy useful items at the Blood Sweat Tears Store, which are usually next to Mission Boards. Such examples are at Saint Haven and Red Lotus Palace Main Street.

  • Hero's Blood Sweat Tears Store

    The Blood Sweat Tear Store [BST Store] is where you can exchange your obtained BST points from missions into several useful items which are not limited to Potential Talisman Slots [Which can be used to expand your Talisman Slots] , Horn of Life [Extend your minion/pet duration] and many more.
