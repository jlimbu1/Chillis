Push Crystal Statue!

How the hell do people get so much HP, you wonder? No worries, the Alliance Jade is here to rescue!

Push Crystal Statue (PCS) is a special Guild-only instance that drops Alliance Crystals, which are used to craft and enhance Alliance Jade at the Blacksmith.

Requirements to run PCS

  1. A party of 6 people, all guild members.
  2. There is NO TICKET for PCS — you can run it freely with your guild members!
  3. It can only be run once a week; however, you can retry infinitely if you fail.

Alliance Jade

The Alliance Jade is a Dragon Jade that can only be equipped on Armor, and is one of the main reasons why you see some people having A LOT of HP and taking many more hits from bosses. However, it isn't seen as very good to use until it is +12.
