Push Crystal Statue!

  • Push Crystal Statue!

    How the hell do people get so much HP, you wonder? No worries, the Alliance Jade is here to rescue!

    Push Crystal Statue (PCS) is a special Guild-only instance that drops Alliance Crystals, which are used to craft and enhance Alliance Jade at the Blacksmith.

    • Requirements to run PCS
    • 1) Party of 6 people, all guild members
    • 2)There is NO TICKET for PCS, you can run it freely with your guild members!
    • 3) Can only be ran once a week, however can retry infinite times if fail

  • Alliance Jade

    The Alliance Jade is a Dragon Jade that can only be equipped on Armors, and is one of the main reasons why you see some people having ALOT of HP and taking much more hits from bosses. However it isn't seen as very good to use until it is +12.
