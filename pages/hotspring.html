Hot Spring

Inside the Hot Spring, there will be a progress bar above your skill bar.

  • S1: 100 FTG
  • S2: 300 FTG
  • S3: 600 FTG
  • (Takes 20 minutes, 13 minutes with a fairy)

Stretching with other players by pressing spacebar on them will increase the progress bar for recovering FTG.
