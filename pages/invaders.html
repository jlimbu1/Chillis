Invaders

Invaders are bosses that appear randomly at any LB6+ Nest or Dungeon before the final stage of said instance. There are differences between Nest Invaders and Dungeon Invaders that separate the two.

Dungeon Invaders

There are two types of Dungeon Invaders: Normal Invaders and Tomb Invaders. The chance of both of them appearing will increase when you have any mission for that specific Dungeon that you are currently running; however, Tomb Invaders REQUIRE a mission for a chance for it to appear, and its rewards scale on the Labyrinth Level that you run the Dungeon on.

Additionally, when defeated, Tomb Invaders have a chance to drop one of the three Skill Jades (Dreamy, Blood Moon, Verdure), and the chances of each Skill Jade dropping scale on the type of gravestone that spawned and the current Labyrinth level. Fragments, however, are a guaranteed drop and can be collected to craft a Sealed Skill Jade at the Blacksmith once enough are collected.

Nest Invaders

There are 3 types of Nest Invaders which are distinguishable by their colours and ascending rarity: Grey, Blue, Purple. These invaders have shared common rewards as well as additional specialized rewards specific to their colour.
