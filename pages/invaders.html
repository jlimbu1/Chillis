Invaders

  • Invaders

    Invaders are bosses that appear randomly at any LB6+ Nest or Dungeon before the final stage of said instance. There are differences between Nest Invaders and Dungeon Invaders that separate the two.

  • Dungeon Invaders

    There are two types of Dungeon Invaders ; Normal Invaders & Tomb Invaders. The chance of both of them appearing will increase when you have any mission for that specific Dungeon that you are currently running however Tomb Invaders REQUIRE a mission for a chance for it to appear, and its rewards scale on the Labyrinth Level that you run the Dungeon on. Additionally, Tomb Invaders when defeated, have chance to drop one of the three Skill Jades [Dreamy , Blood Moon , Verdure] and chances of each Skill Jade to drop scales on the TYPE OF GRAVESTONE that spawned and current LABYRINTH level. Fragments, however are a guaranteed drop and can be collected to craft a Sealed Skill Jade at the Blacksmith once enough are collected.

  • Nest Invaders

    There are 3 types of Nest Invaders which are distinguishable by their colours and ascending rarity : Grey , Blue , Purple. These invaders have shared common rewards as well as additional specialized rewards specific to its colour.

    The conditions for them to appear are similar to the previously mentioned Dungeon Invaders.
